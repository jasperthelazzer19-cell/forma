## Headline numbers

- **Total entries:** 68

- **Released TIR forms (2005-2023):** 61
- **Preparing for the ACT booklets:** 7
- **Entries with direct PDF URL:** 11

## Key insight

Per McElroy Tutoring + Quantum: ACT has only ~9 *truly unique* tests in active circulation. The Preparing-for-the-ACT booklets recycle past released forms (e.g. 72CPRE = Form 72C, 74FPRE = Form 74F). Most direct PDF links to TIR exams were taken down after a 2022-ish legal notice from ACT to McElroy. Form codes still let you Google-find most PDFs.

## Gaps / what's missing

- **57 released forms have no direct PDF in this inventory.** McElroy removed them after ACT legal notice. To find them: Google the form code (e.g. `"Form 72E" filetype:pdf`) — often surfaces on tutor sites, Reddit, Discord exports, or school district sites.
- **Form codes I could not verify against multiple sources:** Z04, Z08, Z15, Z18 (Quantum lists these as April supplemental forms; may overlap with main April forms of those years).
